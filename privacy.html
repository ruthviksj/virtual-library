POL 001.01 PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026

In short: this site has no accounts, no cookies, no ads and no analytics. Most of what it remembers stays in your own browser. The only thing a visitor ever sends to me is a book recommendation they choose to submit.

01Who runs this site

Rushi's Library is a personal website by Rushi, who catalogues the books they read. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. In this policy, "I" and "me" mean Rushi, and "you" means anyone visiting the site.

02What stays in your browser

The site uses your browser's local storage, not cookies, to remember a few things on your own device:

  • the room colour you picked and which shelves you opened with "Show all";
  • book cover links and spine colours it has already looked up, so pages load faster next time.

None of this is sent to me. You can clear it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

03Recommending a book

If you use Recommend a book, the book you picked and the name you type are sent to me by email through Web3Forms, a form-delivery service. Please don't enter anything you would not want me to read.

If I add your recommendation, the book and the name you gave appear publicly on the Recommended to Me shelf, as "Recommended by" that name. Use a first name or nickname if you prefer. If you would like a recommendation or your name removed, contact me (see the last section) and I will take it down.

04Services the page talks to

To work, your browser connects directly to a few outside services. Each one receives the basic details any website receives, such as your IP address and browser type, under its own privacy policy:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the site;
  • Google Fonts, for the typefaces;
  • Google Books and Open Library, to look up book details and covers (only the book titles being searched or shown are sent);
  • images.weserv.nl, sometimes used to read a cover's colour for the spine;
  • Web3Forms, only when you send a recommendation.

I do not use these services to track you, and I do not receive any visitor data from them.

05What I don't do

  • No sign-ups, accounts or passwords for visitors.
  • No cookies, advertising, tracking pixels or analytics.
  • No selling or sharing of anything you send me.

06For the library's owner

The editing tools save the owner's working copy of the library and their GitHub publishing key in the owner's own browser only. The key is sent only to GitHub, to publish the library.

07Children

This site is not aimed at children. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before sending a recommendation with your name.

08Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. For questions or removal requests, email contact@sjruthvik.in.
